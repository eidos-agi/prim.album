# Why prim.album exists

An mp3 is a frozen take. A jukebox is a program. Neither is a store an agent can open, validate, and hand to another tool.

**prim.album is one record as a file.** Title, artist, liner notes, ordered tracks. Audio files and generative patches are sources the tracks cite. The player is a projection.

If a field is in this spec, a cabinet failed for lack of it.

- **One album, one track list.** A single is still an album. Size-1 collections are valid.
- **`album.json` is authority.** HTML/WebAudio/mp3 are views or cited sources.
- **Cite audio. Do not become the song.** A licensed mix does not belong in the pack as the store.
- **Do not mint `prim.track`.** A track is a record on the album. It travels with the record.
- **Do not confuse with `prim.deck`.** That is slides. The arcade jukebox is a player that *cites* this prim.
